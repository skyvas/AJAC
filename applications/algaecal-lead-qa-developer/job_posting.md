## Overview
AlgaeCal is seeking a Lead QA Developer / Automation QA Engineer (Lead) to drive quality engineering across their eCommerce platform, React-based storefront, and internal middleware services. The role involves designing robust test automation architecture, integrating automated test suites with CI/CD pipelines, and mentoring engineering team members.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead the architectural design, implementation, and maintenance of automated testing frameworks (Playwright, Cypress, JavaScript/TypeScript).
- Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines to ensure continuous quality feedback and reduce flaky tests.
- Define QA metrics, establish testing best practices, and lead test planning for releases.
- Mentor and coach software engineers and QA team members on test automation principles and clean test code.
- Collaborate with product management, UX, and backend development teams to deliver reliable software.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in software test automation and QA development.
- 2+ years of leadership, mentorship, or coaching experience.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript and TypeScript.
- Hands-on expertise building UI and API automation frameworks with Playwright, Cypress, or Selenium.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins) and version control.
