# Job Posting: Quality Assurance Engineer

- **Company**: Yocale (Yocale Network Corporation)
- **Title**: Quality Assurance Engineer (QA Automation Engineer)
- **Location**: North Vancouver, BC, Canada (390 – 889 Harbourside Drive, North Vancouver, BC)
- **Source URL**: https://www.yocale.com/careers
- **Date Collected**: 2026-08-19
- **Remote Policy**: On-site / Hybrid (North Vancouver, BC)
- **Seniority**: Intermediate (3+ years QA automation experience)

## Overview
Yocale is an all-in-one business management and online scheduling SaaS platform empowering appointment-based businesses with booking, client management, integrated payments/POS, notifications, and workflow automation. Yocale is seeking a QA Automation Engineer to design, build, and maintain automated test frameworks and support release quality across their web and mobile applications.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and develop scalable test automation frameworks to support web and mobile releases.
- Create detailed, comprehensive, and well-structured automated test plans and test cases based on application, performance, and regression requirements.
- Oversee and implement QA best practices across the software engineering lifecycle.
- Collaborate with Product Managers, Product Owners, and Developers to improve upcoming software releases.
- Perform functional, performance, and regression testing while reporting and tracking defects in Jira.
- Troubleshoot issues and conduct root cause analysis for identified defects.
- Assist in deployment verification to production environments and maintain automated test execution infrastructure.

## Qualifications & Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in software quality assurance and automated test development.
- Experience in a fast-paced SaaS company preferred.
- Familiarity or comfort with React and React Native web and mobile architectures.
- Strong knowledge of software testing fundamentals (test plan design, test case creation, regression workflows).
- Experience setting up and maintaining automated test suites and CI/CD integration.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively within an Agile team.
