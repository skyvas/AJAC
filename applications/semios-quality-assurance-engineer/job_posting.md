## Responsibilities

- Own QA processes throughout the software development lifecycle, creating
  and executing test plans
- Perform continuous testing alongside developers during product delivery
- Write test plans, cases, and defect reports with clarity
- Execute regression, functional, exploratory, and smoke testing
- Collaborate with product, development, and support teams to ensure
  specifications are understood
- Help reduce manual regression testing through improved strategies and
  tools

## Must-have requirements

- 2-4 years professional QA or software testing experience
- Solid testing principles and tools foundation
- Ability to work independently and own team testing deliverables
- Cross-functional collaboration skills
- Test case design and execution capability based on requirements
- Bug reporting proficiency using tools like Jira
- Manual testing confidence across multiple testing types

## Nice-to-have

- Knowledge of automated testing tools (Selenium, Postman, Buildkite, Git)
- Experience with AI tools such as Claude Code, Gemini, or Copilot
- Ability to execute and maintain existing test scripts
