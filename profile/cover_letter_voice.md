# Cover letter voice & style guide

Notes reflecting Akash Vashista's professional voice and positioning.
`/apply` should match this voice rather than inventing a new one each time.

## Structure (5 paragraphs)
1. **Opening** — role + company name, one line on years of experience (6+ years in QA automation & software testing), one line on genuine interest in what the company does (specific, not generic flattery).
2. **Track record** — 2-3 sentences summarizing the breadth of relevant experience (designing scalable UI/API test automation frameworks in Playwright/Selenium, Postman API testing, cloud & container testing on AWS/Docker/K8s, CI/CD integration, AI-assisted QA), pitched at the level of the target role.
3. **Why this company** — the one paragraph that MUST change per application. Name a specific product, technology, or mission detail from the job posting/company site and connect it to what Akash enjoys working on. This is where fabrication risk is highest — only reference things confirmed in the job posting or company site.
4. **Personal working style** — analytical mindset, attention to detail, clear communication, robust defect reporting, eagerness to explore modern testing paradigms. If the posting emphasizes automation, API/security testing, or AI-assisted QA, this is the spot to mention relevant project experience from `profile/profile.yaml` (e.g. AI-agent test acceleration, GraphQL/REST frameworks, or GitHub repo: `https://github.com/skyvas`) with a concise clause tying it to the posting. Only include it when it genuinely strengthens the fit; skip it for postings where it wouldn't land.
5. **Closing** — thank them, express willingness to discuss further, sign "Sincerely," + full name ("Akash Vashista").

## Tone
- Confident but not boastful. States facts ("6+ years", "45% reduction in manual effort") rather than superlatives ("world-class").
- Warm, direct, technical, short sentences, no corporate fluff.
- Willing to admit being new to a specific niche tool/domain while framing it as eagerness to quickly adapt.
- Never use em dashes or overly formal phrasing ("herein", "aforementioned").

## Length
4-6 short paragraphs, should fit on one page in the Word template.
